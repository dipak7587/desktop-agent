# Miniature video brief template

Use this brief with Miniature Video Studio:

```text
Platform:
Duration:
Subject/product:
Audience:
Goal or CTA:
Miniature world or scale cue:
Visual style:
Video generator:
Trend link, sound, screenshot, or creator reference:
Brand colors and words to include:
Words, logos, people, or styles to avoid:
```

The agent can create a complete package without a trend link, but it will label
unverified trend ideas as hypotheses. Supply a link or screenshot when you need
the package to follow a specific current format.
